A dynamic Excel dashboard for managing accounts receivable, built on a proper data model rather than ad-hoc spreadsheet formulas. The model structures invoices, customers, and payment records so aging analysis and outstanding balances update reliably as data changes.

VBA automation handles the reporting layer: refreshing source data, regenerating the dashboard views, and producing consistent reports without manual effort. The design draws directly on my audit experience at PwC, where understanding receivable risk was part of the job.

The result is a tool that finance staff can run themselves, with the numbers they need surfaced automatically and clearly.
