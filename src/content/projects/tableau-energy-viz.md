An interactive Tableau dashboard exploring global energy consumption trends across countries and time periods. The visualization combines calculated fields and LOD expressions to enable comparisons at different granularities, from global aggregates down to individual country trajectories.

Time-series analysis was central to the design: the dashboard highlights long-term consumption patterns, growth rates, and structural shifts in energy mixes. Interactivity lets users filter and drill into the story behind the numbers rather than reading a static chart.

The project sharpened my skills in Tableau's more advanced feature set — particularly level-of-detail expressions — and in designing dashboards that answer questions instead of just displaying data.
