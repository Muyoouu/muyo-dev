Nineants is a SaaS platform for automating business workflows, and I own it end to end. As the developer at Automation Boutique, I architected the entire product from the ground up: backend APIs, the React frontend, and the infrastructure that keeps everything running in production.

The technical stack centers on Python services, a React application, and MongoDB, containerized with Docker and deployed to Azure. Beyond writing features, the work has meant making the kind of architectural decisions that shape a product long term — data modeling, service boundaries, and deployment strategy.

Because the platform serves real users, my responsibilities extend past the codebase. I handle deployment, monitoring, production issues, and user onboarding, which keeps the feedback loop between engineering decisions and customer outcomes tight.
